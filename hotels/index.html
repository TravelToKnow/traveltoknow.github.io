Hotel booking checklist

  • Verify property name, address, room category and guest count.
  • Compare the final payable amount, including taxes and mandatory fees.
  • Read cancellation, no-show, prepayment and refund conditions.
  • Check check-in/check-out times and identification requirements.
  • Save the supplier confirmation after booking.
