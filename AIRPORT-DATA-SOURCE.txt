Travel To Know airport dropdown

The Flights From/To dropdowns are populated from the OurAirports open-data airports.csv dataset at runtime.
Source: https://ourairports.com/data/
Direct dataset: https://davidmegginson.github.io/ourairports-data/airports.csv

Only records with a valid 3-letter IATA code are shown. The dataset is updated daily by OurAirports.
A small fallback list of common airports is included so the form remains usable if the external dataset cannot be reached.
